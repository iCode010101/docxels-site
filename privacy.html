Privacy policy

How Docxels handles your documents

Last updated: 14 October 2026 (updates and device check)

Draft text for review. Have a qualified professional check it against your actual practices and local law before relying on it.

1. Who we are

Docxels is a product of Pixels Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Docxels app and this website.

2. Your documents stay on your device

Documents you scan, create, import or edit are stored on your phone, in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Scanning, text recognition, editing and PDF export work offline. We do not receive your documents, we have no user accounts, and the app contains no advertising or analytics. The app asks for internet access for one purpose only: when you tap "Check for updates" in Settings, it reads a small version file and, if you choose, downloads the new app file from the Docxels release page hosted on GitHub. Nothing about you or your documents is sent in that request, but, as with any web request, the host can see your device's IP address. The app never connects to the internet otherwise. Downloaded updates are accepted only if their fingerprint matches and they are signed with the same key as the installed app. Temporary working copies of PDFs are erased when you leave the tool, and the app shows a local-only warning if it detects a rooted or debugged phone (nothing is reported anywhere).

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan documents. Photos are used only when you take or pick them.
  • Files and photos: to import documents and pictures you choose, and to save or share exports where you decide.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: only if you turn on app lock. Android's own prompt does the check; Docxels never receives your biometric data or PIN, only whether the check passed.

4. Sharing and exports

When you export or share a document it goes only where you send it, using Android's share sheet or a folder you choose. Once a file is outside the app, its protection depends on where you put it.

5. AI features, cloud backup and sync

These are not part of the app today. If we add them, they will be optional and off by default, we will update this policy first, and we will ask for your consent before any document leaves your phone.

6. Security

We use the platform's secure storage for the encryption key, keep document contents out of logs, check files before opening them, and switch off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Deleting a document in the app removes its pages from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Files you exported to Downloads or Pictures are yours to delete.

8. Children

Docxels is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests: docxels@pixelslimited.ng.
