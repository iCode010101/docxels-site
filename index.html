Questions

Short, honest answers.

Does Docxels upload my documents?

No. Scanning, cleaning, editing and text recognition all run on your phone, and the app uses the internet only when you ask it to check for updates.

Can I turn a scan into a Word file?

Yes. Docxels reads the page with on-device OCR and opens it as an editable document. OCR can make mistakes, so check important text.

Will my Word files look the same?

Docxels reads and writes standard .docx files. Simple documents carry across well; complex layouts such as text boxes or tracked changes are not fully supported yet. Footnotes are kept as a numbered list of notes at the end.

What if I lose my phone?

Pages are encrypted on the phone and you can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. These protections help against someone reaching the stored files; they cannot protect a phone that is already unlocked.
