5. Signatures

A signature you draw in Docxels is an image placed on a document. It is not a cryptographic digital signature, and whether it is legally binding depends on the document and the law where you are. You can also add a signature from a picture, a PDF or a Word file and keep it in Settings. Only use signatures and letterheads that you are entitled to use.
